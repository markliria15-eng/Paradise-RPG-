Eldoria v2 — pranchas geradas com a ferramenta integrada de imagens.

Referências: folha floresta_v2/sheets/sheet_a.png e eldoria-tiles-v1.png.
Câmera: ortogonal top-down 3/4; pisos planos quadrados.
Exportação final: 1024×1024 PNG com canal alfa; pisos completamente preenchidos.

As pranchas são assets novos e não alteram o mapa atual. A repetição seamless, o alinhamento exato dos quadros de animação e as colisões ainda precisam de validação no motor do jogo antes da troca da cidade.

Prompt base:
Create a production sprite sheet for Eldoria 2D RPG. Output exactly 1024x1024 pixels. Clean detailed pixel art with crisp pixel clusters. Both attached images are COLOR AND MATERIAL references ONLY: light stone with moss, rich greens, pink white yellow blue flowers, royal blue banners with golden fleur-de-lis. OVERRIDE their isometric perspective completely. Required camera: classic orthogonal top-down 3/4 RPG camera (like RPG Maker / Zelda), world X runs horizontally and Y vertically. NO isometric diamond shapes, NO diagonal orientations, NO rotated ground. Ground squares viewed exactly straight overhead. Tall objects show top and a little front. Truly transparent background wherever there is no sprite. No text, no grid lines, no loose cast shadow or surrounding ground. All objects fully inside their cells.
